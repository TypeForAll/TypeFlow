How it works

VideoBuddy matches your words to helpful search ideas, then opens real YouTube results. Voice input depends on your browser. YouTube search pages cannot reliably play inside an embedded player, so this version opens the actual results instead.

If the microphone doesn't work, type your search or tap a topic.
