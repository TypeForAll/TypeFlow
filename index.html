Compatibility
Currently compatible with iOS. Designed for mobile devices only.

Add TypeFlow to Home Screen

  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap Safari’s Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name as TypeFlow, then tap Add.

Once added, tap the TypeFlow icon on your Home Screen to open it like an app.
